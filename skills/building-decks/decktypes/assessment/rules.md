# Rules for an assessment readout deck

**Purpose.** Walk the customer through what the assessment found and what to do next. It is based only on what the customer told us and gave us.

**Outline** (drop what the notes do not support): title, agenda, section "where the customer stands" with bullets and strengths and gaps (`two_column`), three focus areas (`cards`), section "recommended approach" with `process`, key numbers (`stats`), customer cases, `timeline`, closing.

- Customer cases only from references the user provides. Never invent a customer or an outcome.
- Numbers and dates exactly as in the notes. Budget and price figures only if the notes state them.
- Business level: no IP addresses, instance types, account numbers or configuration values.
- One message per slide, short lines. Detail belongs in the written assessment.
