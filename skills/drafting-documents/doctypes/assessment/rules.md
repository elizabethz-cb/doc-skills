# Rules for an assessment report

**Purpose.** Give the customer an honest picture of the current situation, what matters most, and what to do next. It is based only on what the customer told us and gave us, not on hands-on inspection, and it says so in the scope.

**Detail level.** More detail than a SOW is fine (findings and recommendations are the product), but:
- No account numbers, keys, IP addresses or exact configuration values.
- Recommendations say what to do and why, not the exact steps or settings.

**Findings.**
- Group by area: security, reliability, cost, operations, performance.
- Each finding states what was found, based on which input, and why it matters. If it is only an assumption, say so in the finding text and also raise an open question.
- Severity: high = likely to cause an outage, data loss or breach; medium = a real weakness with a workaround; low = improvement. Do not mark high without evidence in the notes.
- Effort: S = days, M = weeks, L = months. A rough guess is fine, and say so when it is one.

**Scores.** 1 to 5 per area only when the notes give enough evidence. Otherwise write "Not assessed" and raise an open question.

**Next steps.** Priority order, maximum six, each a clear action.

**Tone.** Neutral and constructive. Never blame people or suppliers.
